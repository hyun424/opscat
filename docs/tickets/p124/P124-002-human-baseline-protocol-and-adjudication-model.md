# P124-002: human baseline protocol and adjudication model

## Goal

Define human baseline collection and adjudication for future offline
evaluation.

## Contract

- Record reviewer count, role, blind status, case coverage, conflicts, and
  adjudication.
- Report inter-rater agreement and uncertainty.
- Reject self-reviewed or denominator-free baselines.

## Acceptance

Future reports can compare OpsCat judgment quality to a traceable human
baseline without claiming operator replacement.

## Stop Rules

Stop if baseline evidence is self-reviewed, reviewer metadata is absent, or
human comparison is marketed as replacement authority.
