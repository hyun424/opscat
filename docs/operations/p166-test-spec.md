# P166 Test Spec

- Seal truth separately and prove prediction commitment precedes truth opening.
- Include healthy negatives and each supported fault family.
- Verify precursor and incident scoring plus lead-time calculation.
- Reject missing citations, future leakage, altered truth, and aggregate
  tampering.
- Require citation validity 1.0, root-cause top-3 1.0, precursor recall at least
  0.80, and false-positive rate at most 0.05.
- Produce P166 evidence chained to canonical P165 evidence.
