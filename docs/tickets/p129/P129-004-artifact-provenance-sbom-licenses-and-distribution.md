# P129-004: artifact provenance, SBOM, licenses, and distribution

## Goal

Plan mature distribution artifacts and provenance.

## Contract

Require artifact hashes, provenance/signature status, SBOM, license inventory,
release notes, and limitation links.

## Acceptance

Future distribution artifacts are traceable and license-auditable.

## Stop Rules

Stop on missing SBOM, unknown license, missing provenance, or unsupported
release claim.
