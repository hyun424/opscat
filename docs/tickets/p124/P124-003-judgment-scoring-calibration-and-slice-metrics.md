# P124-003: judgment scoring, calibration, and slice metrics

## Goal

Define scoring and reporting for future judgment-quality evaluations.

## Contract

- Score correctness, evidence grounding, causal precision, uncertainty,
  actionability without execution, abstention, and safety.
- Report denominators, confidence intervals, per-slice metrics, and failures.
- Reject aggregate-only or cherry-picked results.

## Acceptance

Future quality reports can show calibrated, slice-aware judgment results with
clear limits.

## Stop Rules

Stop if reports omit denominators, uncertainty, failure cases, or scope
limitations.
