# P114 development results

## Status

P114 deterministic candidate generation passed its consumed-development gate.
The constrained LLM adjudicator did not pass its separate development gate and
was excluded from the authoritative path. The later frozen deterministic
RE2-OB acceptance passed; see `docs/operations/p114-final-summary.md`.

## LLM adjudicator result

The contract was progressively reduced from free-form narrative to a sealed
hypothesis-ID selection. Evidence IDs are now attached by the selected immutable
hypothesis rather than regenerated by the model. Three NVIDIA models were tested
twice on the same six-fault stratified consumed subset:

| Model | Raw contract | Repeat agreement | Joint Top-1 delta | Gate |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Nemotron 3 Nano 30B | 100% / 100% | 83.33% | -16.67pp / 0pp | fail |
| Nemotron 3 Super 120B | 100% / 100% | 100% | -16.67pp / -16.67pp | fail |
| Nemotron 3 Ultra 550B | 100% / 100% | 50% | 0pp / -16.67pp | fail |

An additional rank/score-blinded Nano experiment was worse: joint delta was
-50pp in both runs and agreement was 33.33%. This indicates an evidence
representation/decision problem rather than a simple model-size problem.

## Stop decision

The pre-blind adjudicator gate requires both runs to have raw contract at least
95%, nonnegative joint delta, evidence precision at least 95%, zero authority
violations, and repeat agreement at least 90%. No model satisfied all checks.
The governed next step is deterministic evidence-model improvement and broader
cross-validated development evaluation, not opening RE2-OB or weakening gates.

That next step produced a service-name-free robust 3-NN fault classifier. Its
leave-one-root-service-out result was 73.33% fault accuracy and 65.56% joint
Top-1, passing the pre-acceptance development gate. The authoritative blind
candidate is therefore deterministic; see
`docs/operations/p114-deterministic-acceptance-amendment.md`.
