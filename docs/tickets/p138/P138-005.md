# P138-005 - Exact 30-case real-boundary runner

## Scope

Write failing runner/profile/freeze tests, then implement the deterministic
local runner, source-bound fixture profile, reused P136/P137 builders, and exact
immutable 30-case matrix from the roadmap.

## Acceptance

- The matrix contains exactly `P138-CASE-01` through `P138-CASE-30` with the
  roadmap's exact scenario and terminal/post-state meaning; CASE-07 is the
  crash after publisher intent fsync.
- Every row freezes input hash, expected phase path, status/error, stop reason,
  exact actual/expected authority/runtime/evaluator maps, resources, and
  required durable post-state.
- CASE-21 through CASE-25 and CASE-29 through CASE-30 execute real durable
  boundaries. At least CASE-02, CASE-03, CASE-07, CASE-09, CASE-10, CASE-16,
  and CASE-18 also use real applicable P136/publisher/P137 boundaries rather
  than callback-only spies.
- Coverage includes reconciliation and no-work; every phase crash; all three
  publisher split-commit crash points; P138/P136/publisher/P137 contention;
  fork/chain/state mismatch; genesis rejection; both sides of P136 two-phase
  outcome recovery; exact stop labels/cadence/readiness/signals; and
  authority/leak/resource guards.
- P136/P137 fixtures are reused without copying or re-claiming their release
  denominators.
- Preliminary mode writes the tracked matrix, manifest, and preliminary
  evidence under `evals/p138/output`; final mode consumes those same inputs and
  `evals/p138/final-implementation-review.json` without regeneration.
- Runner stdout contains exact `release_status`, `mode`, and `evidence_hash`.
  CASE-29 and CASE-30 prove two-phase P136 outcome recovery before checkpoint
  replacement for nonempty and empty cycles. Runner/profile/freeze checks
  require 30 expected, 30 passed, and 0 failed.
- Targeted runner/profile/freeze tests, Ruff, and Mypy pass before P138-006.
