# P130-005: verification handoff, exit, and rollback gates

## Goal

Plan final P130 verification handoff, exit, rollback, and claim withdrawal.

## Contract

Handoff lists evidence, limitations, risks, feedback process, exit criteria,
rollback, claim withdrawal, and exact-zero counters.

## Acceptance

Future beta promotion or withdrawal is evidence-governed.

## Stop Rules

Stop if handoff omits withdrawal path, limitations, risks, or authority
counters.
