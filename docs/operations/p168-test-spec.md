- Run an accelerated unattended soak over at least 100 virtual cycles.
- Restart from a checkpoint without duplicated evidence or actions.
- Trip the kill switch and deadman and prove no action escapes.
- Inject successful and harmful remediations and close every effect.

  counters, and no unresolved effects.
