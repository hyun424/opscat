  replacement, publisher lease and all three split-commit recoveries, P137,
  P138 reconciliation/phase/bootstrap/delta behavior, exact contracts/counters,
  runner, profile, fixtures, all 30 cases, tests, and docs with zero unresolved
  P0/P1/P2 findings.
- Preliminary and final operate on the exact tracked matrix, manifest, and
  evidence paths under `evals/p138/output`; final consumes
  `evals/p138/final-implementation-review.json` and regenerates no reviewed
  input.
- Final evidence records all case IDs and exactly 30 expected/30 passed/0
  failed, exact rebuilt counter maps, resource usage, source/hash scope,
  reviewer separation, component statuses, commands, and explicit limitations.
- `p138-release` passes against the frozen paths, followed by separate
  `p136-release`, `p137-release`, docs, full verification, authority/leak/
  secret/path checks, and `git diff --check`.
- Release status is exactly
  `p138_local_observation_to_triage_supervisor_qualified` and does not broaden
  the no-authority or local finite-supervisor claim.
- The handoff remains explicitly pending until the independent final
  implementation review, frozen 30/30 evidence, and every verification result
  above exist; plan approval alone is not final qualification evidence.
- Withdraw the release claim if reproduction fails, reviewed hashes drift, the
  matrix is not 30/30, any counter map differs from its exact schema, or any
  forbidden-authority value becomes nonzero.
